# Product

Written from CLAUDE.md, CONTEXT.md, plan/04-design-system.md and the user's review feedback of 3 Oct 2026 (plan/05-build-plan.md). Not yet confirmed line by line with the user.

## Register

product

The landing page at / is the one brand surface. Everything inside the shell is product.

## Users

- An accountant or finance lead at an Indian small or mid-sized business, closing the month. They know GST and distrust tools that hide how a number was reached.
- For the event: a judge seeing LedgerLens for the first time, in a 2 to 2.5 minute video and then live on a projector at 1440x900 or 1920x1080. They must understand the problem and the answer without help.

## Product purpose

Reconcile one month of books end to end: price every mismatch as ITC at risk or ITC found, explain each Finding with evidence, offer a Draft fix to approve in one click, and catch Supplier rings on the network view. Success is a first-time viewer who can say what the month costs, why, and what to do next.

## Brand personality

Exact, plain-spoken, accountable. It reads like a careful accountant's working paper, not a pitch. Confidence comes from showing the working, not from decoration.

## Anti-references

The user's words: "everything looks too much AI".

- Grids of icon, title and text cards.
- Tinted pills and chips on everything.
- Every block boxed in a rounded card.
- All information at one weight, nothing leading.
- A dark floating tooltip bar that looks generated.
- Marketing voice, invented statistics, anything that looks better than the data supports.

## Design principles

1. Rupees first. The three money numbers lead; everything else explains them.
2. Show the working. Every number can be traced to records and a rule.
3. One thing leads on each screen; the rest is revealed in reading order.
4. Honest about the data. It is synthetic, with planted errors and an answer key, and the misses are shown.
5. Structure with type, rules and space before reaching for a box or a colour.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Text contrast at least 4.5:1. Status always has a text label as well as colour. All actions reachable by keyboard; the drawer traps focus and closes on Escape. Charts have a text summary. Reduced motion respected. Desktop only (mobile is out of scope).
